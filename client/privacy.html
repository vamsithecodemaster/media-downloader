Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 20, 2026

Welcome to MediaGrab. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, in compliance with standard data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

MediaGrab is designed to function with minimal data collection. However, certain interactions require underlying technical data:

  • Log Data & Server Information: When you use MediaGrab, our servers automatically record standard log information including your IP address, browser type, and timestamps of media conversion requests.
  • Local Storage (Cookies & Cache): We use your browser's local storage (LocalStorage) to save your recent download history locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Media URLs: Links submitted to our service are processed ephemerally on our server to fetch and download the requested media. They are not permanently tied to your personal identity.

2. Third-Party Ad Networks and Analytics

To keep MediaGrab free, we may partner with third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense) and analytics providers. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Serve personalized ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns to improve our service.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (Your Rights)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have certain data protection rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

Because MediaGrab mostly processes data anonymously and stores user history purely locally, we do not have an account system to delete. To clear your local data, you may use the "Clear History" button on the main app or clear your browser cache/cookies.

4. Data Retention

Files downloaded using MediaGrab are held temporarily on our servers for the duration necessary for you to save the file. A completely automated script securely scrubs all downloaded media from our servers shortly after processing (typically within 1 hour). We do not permanently store downloaded media.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy or your data, do not hesitate to contact us at privacy@mediagrab.in.
