Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you. Get in touch with the MediaGrab team.

Whether you have a question about how MediaGrab works, want to report a bug, need help with a download, or just want to share feedback — we're always happy to hear from our users. Here are the best ways to reach us:

Email

support@mediagrab.in

We aim to respond within 24-48 hours.

Feedback

Feature requests and suggestions are welcome!

Email us at feedback@mediagrab.in

Frequently Asked Questions

Before reaching out, check if your question is already answered in our FAQ section. We cover common topics like supported platforms, download issues, quality options, and more.

Report a Bug

Found something not working correctly? We appreciate bug reports! When reporting a bug, please include:

  • The URL you were trying to download
  • The browser and device you're using
  • A description of what happened vs. what you expected
  • Any error messages you received

Send bug reports to bugs@mediagrab.in and we'll investigate as soon as possible.

Business Inquiries

For advertising, partnerships, or business-related questions, please contact us at business@mediagrab.in.

Legal

For DMCA takedown requests, copyright concerns, or legal matters, please email legal@mediagrab.in. We take all legal inquiries seriously and will respond within 48 hours.
